Inside Planets & Brown Dwarfs

A simple look at what different kinds of worlds are made of inside.

Rocky Planets (Earth, Mars)

  • Crust: - Solid rock surface, - Very thin outer shell
  • Mantle: - Thick layer of hot rock, - Slowly flows over time
  • Outer Core: - Liquid metal (iron & nickel), - Creates the magnetic field
  • Inner Core: - Solid metal ball, - Super hot and under high pressure
  • Mass - 0.1 - 40 Earth Mass
  • Learn more

Gas Giants (Jupiter, Saturn)

  • Atmosphere: - Mostly hydrogen and helium gas, - Colorful cloud bands and storms
  • Upper Ocean: - Thick layer of liquid hydrogen, - Gets denser the deeper you go
  • Deep Mantle: - Liquid metallic hydrogen, - Conducts electricity like metal [Magnetic Field]
  • Core: - Dense mix of rock and ice, - Trapped under crushing weight
  • Mass - 0.1 - 12 Jupiter Mass
  • Learn more

Ice Giants (Uranus, Neptune)

  • Atmosphere: - Hydrogen, helium, and methane, - Methane makes them look blue
  • Mantle: - Thick slush of hot "ices", - Made of water, ammonia, and methane
  • Deep Layers: - Pressure can turn carbon into real diamonds, - Super hot liquid depths
  • Core: - Small core of rock and iron, - About the size of Earth
  • Mass - 10 - 30 Earth Mass
  • Learn more

Brown Dwarfs ("Failed Stars")

  • Atmosphere: - Strange hot skies, - Clouds made of liquid iron or dust
  • Main Body: - Giant ball of hot gas and plasma, - Churns heat from inside out
  • Core Energy: - Ate a little heavy fuel early on, - Too light to shine like a star
  • too heavy to be called a gas giant
  • Center: - Extreme packed matter, - Held up by quantum pressure
  • Mass - 13 - 75 Jupiter Mass

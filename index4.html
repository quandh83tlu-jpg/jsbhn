• Rocky Planets

  • How are they formed?

    • Stage 1: Dust Clumping - Tiny space dust grains bump and stick together into pebbles and boulders
    • Stage 2: Rock Smash-Up - Gravity pulls these boulders into violent collisions, creating miles-wide rocks.
    • Stage 3: Giant Growth! - Tiny space dust grains bump and stick together into pebbles and boulders
    • Stage 4: Cooling Down. - The ball cools, forming a solid outer crust and a heavy metal core.
